---
question: Can I add the training times to my calendar? What if training is cancelled?
---

Yes, as a calendar subscription. When training is cancelled (for example in the holidays), those dates disappear from the subscription by themselves, and every page of the website shows a notice at the top.

- **iPhone, iPad, Mac:** tap a group in the [timetable](/en/#woche), then “Subscribe in your calendar”. Or all groups at once: [subscribe](webcal://www.kuk-sool-won-griepentrog.de/kalender/training.ics).
- **Google Calendar (Android):** on a computer, open Google Calendar, click “+” next to “Other calendars”, choose “From URL” and paste this address: `https://www.kuk-sool-won-griepentrog.de/kalender/training.ics`. For one group, replace “training” with its id, e.g. `.../kalender/ksw-9-11.ics`.
- **Outlook:** “Add calendar” › “Subscribe from web” and paste the same address.

Calendar apps only check a subscription every few hours, Google sometimes only once a day. Short-notice cancellations therefore show on the website first.
