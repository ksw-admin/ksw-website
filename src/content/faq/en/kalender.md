---
question: Can I add the training times to my calendar? What if training is cancelled?
---

Yes, as a calendar subscription. When training is cancelled (for example in the holidays), those dates disappear from the subscription by themselves, and every page of the website shows a notice at the top.

The links are on the [calendar page](/en/dates/): **Google Calendar** or **iPhone, Mac, Outlook**. For one group only, choose the group at the top first; the links then cover just that group.

- **Google Calendar (Android too):** the link opens Google Calendar asking “Add calendar?”. On a phone this works best in the browser; the dates then show in the calendar app as well. If not: in Google Calendar on a computer, click “+” next to “Other calendars” › “From URL” and paste `https://www.kuk-sool-won-griepentrog.de/calendar/training.ics`.
- **iPhone, iPad, Mac, Outlook:** the link opens the calendar app and asks whether to subscribe. In Outlook on the web: “Add calendar” › “Subscribe from web” and paste the same address.

Calendar apps only check a subscription every few hours, Google sometimes only once a day. Short-notice cancellations therefore show on the website first.
