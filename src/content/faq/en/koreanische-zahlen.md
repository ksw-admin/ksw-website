---
question: How do you count in Korean?
---

In Kuk Sool Won we count in Korean. The numbers from 1 to 100:

- **1 to 10:** Hana, Dul, Set, Net, Dasot, Yosot, Ilgob, Yodul, Ahob, Yol.
- **11 to 19:** Yol plus the unit, so Yolhana (11), Yoldul (12), Yolset (13) and so on.
- **Tens:** Seumul (20), Soreun (30), Maheun (40), Swin (50), Yesun (60), Ilheun (70), Yodeun (80), Aheun (90).
- **100:** Bak.
