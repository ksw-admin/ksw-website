---
question: What is Kuk Sool Won?
---

A Korean martial art that combines strikes, throws, joint locks, breakfalls, stick and sword techniques in one system. Breathing training is part of it from the start and supports the powerful yet gentle techniques. In Germany, Kuk Sool Won is still not very widespread. More on the [Kuk Sool Won](/en/category/kuksoolwon/) page.
